EditHere 0.12.3 · Standard macOS drag-and-drop installation

The DMG now opens with just two visible entries: EditHere on the left and Applications on the right, with an arrow showing where to drag the app. Drag EditHere into Applications, launch it from there, and eject the disk image.

- Documentation, licenses, AI skills, schemas, and the version stamp are included inside the app bundle and travel with it.
- The bundled CLI remains at `EditHere.app/Contents/MacOS/edithere-cli`.
- The final DMG is mounted and checked, then the app is copied out and tested after the image is ejected.
- Includes the Dock window restoration and monochrome menu bar icon fixes, with updated whiteboard instructions.

Quit the previous version completely before replacing EditHere in Applications. The macOS app remains ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple; Gatekeeper may require permission to open it. Windows, Linux, and Web packages are also included.
